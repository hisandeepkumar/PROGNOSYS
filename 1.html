About the Company

From Importer to Full-Stack
Imaging Manufacturer

Prognosys Medical Systems designs and manufactures digital medical imaging equipment under the ProRad™ brand. Headquartered in Bengaluru, the company has grown from an importer and distributor of digital X-ray systems into a full-stack engineering and manufacturing organisation.

Today we develop radiology and fluoroscopy solutions for hospitals, clinics, field deployments, and veterinary applications — serving customers globally with dependable, indigenously engineered technology.
